Terms of Service & Travel Advisory

Last updated: August 2026

1. Nature of Platform

RAAHIYOO provides informational and editorial travel discovery resources. RAAHIYOO is not a licensed tour operator, travel agency, or direct ticket vendor. All bookings and financial transactions are conducted externally between travelers and authorized service providers.

2. Mountain Travel & Safety Advisory

Himalayan weather, road accessibility, high-altitude passes, and monsoon conditions can change rapidly without notice. RAAHIYOO strongly advises all travelers to:

  • Verify road and weather conditions with local State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA) and India Meteorological Department (IMD).
  • Acclimatize properly and consult medical professionals before ascending above 3,000 meters.
  • Trek with certified local guides on high alpine wilderness trails.

3. Accuracy of Information

While our editorial team rigorously audits destination facts and transit blueprints, transport timings, state permit regulations, and fees are subject to government revision. Always confirm with official transit sources.
