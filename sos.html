⚠️ High-Altitude Sickness (AMS) Golden Rules

If you or any travel companion experiences headache, dizziness, nausea, chest heaviness, or blue lips above 2,500 meters:

1. Stop Ascending Immediately

Never push higher to sleep or sightsee when symptoms appear.

2. Oxygen & Warm Hydration

Inhale portable oxygen at 2-4 L/min and drink warm water with Electral/ORS.

3. Mandatory Immediate Descent

Descend 500m – 1000m immediately if condition doesn't improve within 2 hours.

4. Army / ITBP Medical Posts

Indian Army and ITBP camps along mountain highways provide free emergency oxygen & first-aid.
