Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information Collection & Use

RAAHIYOO is a free editorial travel discovery platform. We do not require mandatory user accounts, do not harvest personal credentials, and do not track invasive cross-site behavioral cookies.

2. LocalStorage Usage

Features such as the interactive packing checklist store state exclusively within your local browser storage (localStorage). This data remains on your personal device and is not transmitted to external commercial servers.

3. External Links & Third-Party Services

Our travel guides provide links to official transport services (e.g. IRCTC, State Tourism Boards). RAAHIYOO is not responsible for the privacy practices or data policies of external websites.
