Clean Mountain & Coast Pledge

Leave only footprints, take only memories. Protect India's sacred mountains and heritage trails.

🎒
Pack It In, Pack It Out: Bring all non-biodegradable trash back to base towns with authorized municipal waste management.

Support Local Communities: Stay at family-run homestays, hire licensed union drivers, and taste authentic regional foods.

2. Ground Truth Tariffs & Schedules

All transit routes, train timings, and taxi tariffs are curated from official Indian Railways (IRCTC), state transport corporations, and local taxi operator unions. Actual ground tariffs may vary slightly during extreme monsoon landslides, winter snowfall, or peak holiday surges.

3. High-Altitude Safety Advisory

Travelers visiting high Himalayan passes (above 10,000 ft) are strongly advised to acclimatize properly for 48 hours and check real-time BRO (Border Roads Organisation) advisories before embarking.
